GEN-Y VIEW • 1950s → 2026

The Evolution of AI Tree

AI did not arrive with ChatGPT. Today's Generative and Agentic AI are leaves on a tree whose roots include symbolic reasoning, pattern recognition, perception, machine learning and decades of software engineering.

1940s–1950s • Roots

🧠 Computing & Logic

The dawn: turning human logic into math & computational search.

Impact: Turing Test • Symbolic AI • Lisp
1950s–1970s

🔎 Pattern Recognition

Teaching machines to categorize shapes, voice, and early text.

Impact: Perceptron • Early OCR • Classifiers
1960s–1980s

👁️ Perception & Expert Systems

If-Then rules attempt to digitize human master decision-making.

Impact: MYCIN • Visual edge detection
1980s–2000s

📈 Statistical Machine Learning

Ditching hardcoded rules; systems learn directly from data patterns.

Impact: SVMs • Decision Trees • MATLAB
2000s–2012

🕸️ GPU-Powered Neural Nets

Massive data meets parallel processing—neural nets ignite.

Impact: CUDA • ImageNet explosion
2012–2017

🔥 Deep Learning Revolution

Feature engineering dies; end-to-end vision & audio models reign supreme.

Impact: AlexNet • TensorFlow • PyTorch
2017–2021

⚡ Transformers & Foundation Models

Attention mechanism unlocks global context across language and audio.

Impact: Self-Attention • BERT • GPT-3
2022–2026

✨ Autonomous Agentic AI

From answering text to executing multi-step workflows with real-time tool access.

Impact: Gemini • Claude • Agent SDKs • MCP
